Find your way around the shop app

Each picture has numbered spots; the list next to it says what they do. The shop in the pictures is made up. In the app, Settings → Help → Take the tour points at the real buttons.

For the shop owner

Set up once, then most days live on Home, Jobs and Schedule.

  1. Open and sign inGo to garageterminal.com/shop and sign in with Google. Add it to your home screen to use it like an app.
  2. Turn on Shop modeSettings (⚙) → Shop mode. It turns on customer jobs, invoices and the Schedule.
  3. Add your shop infoJobs → ⚙ Settings: shop name, address, phone, labor rate and receipt paper width.
  4. Check in your first jobJobs → + New → Customer job. Write the job number on the tag.
  5. Add your team (optional)Settings → Employees: add each person with their Google email and a role.

Home: your shop's day

Home is where the app opens. It shows what needs you today; tap any card to jump to it.

  1. Timesheet Who is clocked in right now. Tap Open for everyone's hours (shows when the shop clock is on).
  2. Counts Open jobs, jobs ready for pickup, and parts to order. Tap one to go to that list.
  3. Today Today's drop-offs, pickups and deliveries, with times. Tap one to open the job.
  4. This week A dot for each booking. Tap a day to open it on the Schedule.
  5. Coming up Jobs promised in the next 7 days, late ones and jobs ready to go back.
  6. Tabs Home, Jobs, Schedule, Machines, Parts and Tools. Tap the tab you're on to go back to its start.

OWNER 2

Jobs: every job in one list

The Jobs tab is Job_Board. Customer jobs and your own projects live here.

  1. + New Check a machine in. Customer jobs get the next job number (J-014, J-015…).
  2. Shopping list Parts still to order across all jobs, with a count.
  3. Search Find a job by customer, machine, part or note.
  4. Customers Your customer list: phone, address, their machines and every job they've had.
  5. Filters Everyone, Assigned to me, or Not assigned (with employees on).
  6. A job card Progress, the next task, and whether its parts are on your shelf. Tap to open.

OWNER 3

Checking a machine in

Tap + New, then Customer job.

  1. Customer job Customer jobs get a job number, customer info, invoices and the schedule.
  2. Customer Pick a customer from your list, or New customer and type their name and phone.
  3. Their machine Pick one of their machines to keep its history, or type it in.
  4. How it comes in They drop it off or you pick it up, the day and time, the promised date, and how it goes back.

OWNER 4

Working a job

Everything about one job, top to bottom.

  1. Edit job details Change the machine, dates, times and how it comes in and goes back. (Edit customer is further down.)
  2. Machine Opens the machine in Machines, with every visit it's had.
  3. Dates Drop-off, pickup and promised dates. Late dates turn orange.
  4. Status Not started, In progress, Waiting on parts, Ready for pickup, Done. Done jobs file under the customer.
  5. Assigned to Who is working on it. Their initials show on the job card.
  6. Clock in Times the work on this job. The time can go straight onto the invoice as labor.

OWNER 5

Parts, labor and delivery

Further down the job, the money adds up as you go.

  1. Parts total Every part on the job and its tasks. Parts without a price are counted for you.
  2. Labor What was done, hours and rate. Your default rate comes from Shop info.
  3. Use clocked time Fills the hours from the job clock (only approved time, if you approve time).
  4. Check the distance Opens Google Maps from your shop to the customer for a pickup or delivery.
  5. Miles Type the miles; the delivery fee works itself out from your rate in Settings.

OWNER 6

Invoices, reports and receipts

At the bottom of a customer job.

  1. Notes for the customer Prints at the end of the invoice and work report. Project notes stay private.
  2. PDF Save the invoice or work report as a PDF to text or email it.
  3. Print Print a full page straight away.
  4. Receipt For a receipt printer. Pick your paper width (58, 76, 80 or 112 mm) in Shop info.
  5. Shop info Your shop name, address, phone and labor rate. They print at the top of every invoice.

OWNER 7

Schedule

Four weeks at a glance, or the whole month.

  1. Timesheet Who is in, and everyone's hours (owners and managers).
  2. 4 weeks or Month Weeks start on Sunday. Arrows move; Today comes back.
  3. Days Dots: orange = coming in, green = going back, yellow = promised. A full day is underlined.
  4. Legend What the dots mean. Full day follows your daily job limit in Settings.
  5. The day Everything booked on the picked day. Tap one to open the job.
  6. + Schedule Book a new job on the picked day. It opens the new job form with that date filled in.

OWNER 8

Machines

Customers' machines and your own, with specs and history (the Logbook).

  1. Mine / Customers' Your own machines, or customers' machines grouped by customer.
  2. Customer Each customer's machines together.
  3. Specs Engine, spark plug, oil and filters. Part numbers check your shelf.
  4. In your inventory How many of its part numbers you have in stock.
  5. + Add engine Add a machine. Set "Whose is it?" for a customer's machine.

OWNER 9

Parts and inventory

Your shelf, what to reorder and what's on order.

  1. + Add part Add a part with brand, number, location, reorder level and price.
  2. Counts Parts, to reorder, on order. Tap to filter.
  3. Search By name, part number, barcode, other brands' numbers, or what it fits.
  4. Scan Scan a barcode with the camera or a Bluetooth scanner. Bulk scan for a delivery.
  5. Suppliers Your supplier list with lead times, for expected dates on orders.
  6. − / + Change the count. Parts used on jobs come off here from the job.
  7. Cross-reference Spark plugs link to the Spark Plug Finder for other brands' numbers.

OWNER 10

Shop tools

Reference charts that work with no signal once opened.

  1. Torque Chart SAE and metric bolts by grade, dry or oiled.
  2. Fuel Mix 2-stroke oil for any can and ratio.
  3. Spark Plug Finder Any plug number to NGK and other brands, or a machine to its plug and gap.
  4. Tap Drill Chart Drill size for any tap.

OWNER 11

Employees and roles

Settings (⚙, top right) → Employees. Off by default; a one-person shop doesn't need it.

  1. Employees Turn on to add people. Everything stays on your account.
  2. Status Invited until they sign in and tap Join, then Joined.
  3. Send the invite Email or copy the invite. They sign in at garageterminal.com/shop with that Google email.
  4. + Add an employee Name, Google email and role: Tech, Front counter or Manager.
  5. What each role can do Switch on or off prices, contact info, inventory, settings, deleting jobs and approving time per role.

OWNER 12

Timesheet

Home or Schedule → Timesheet. Turn the shop clock on in Settings → Clocks.

  1. This week / Last week Sunday to Saturday.
  2. Hours Hours per person per day, totals and overtime. Tap a day to see or fix the times.
  3. Export CSV For payroll.
  4. OK week Approve the week. Edited days and days nobody clocked out of are flagged first.

For employees

Your shop owner adds you first. Then:

  1. Sign inOpen garageterminal.com/shop and sign in with the Google email your shop owner used.
  2. Tap JoinHome asks to join the shop. After that the app opens your shop's jobs, machines and parts.
  3. Clock inIf your shop uses the shop clock, clock in on Home at the start of the day.
  4. Work your jobsMy jobs on Home lists what's assigned to you. Tick tasks as you finish them.

Your Home screen

After you tap Join, the app opens your shop.

  1. Your shop and role You see what your role allows.
  2. Clock in Clock in when you start the day (if your shop uses the shop clock). Job clocks unlock once you're in.
  3. My hours Your own timesheet.
  4. My jobs Jobs assigned to you, with what's left to do. Tap to open.

On the clock

The card turns green while you're clocked in.

  1. Start break Breaks come off your hours.
  2. Clock out Add a note if you need to. Your job clock stops too.
  3. On jobs / shop time Time on jobs is billable; the rest is shop time.

Working a job

Open a job from My jobs or the Jobs tab.

  1. Status Move it along: In progress, Waiting on parts, Ready for pickup.
  2. Assigned to Who is on it.
  3. Clock in Start the job clock when you work on it, stop it when you switch jobs.
Your own garage too. Settings → Your shop → Switch to my own garage opens your own machines and projects while you stay on the team. Switch back the same way.
