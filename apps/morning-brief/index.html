Where do I find this?
  1. Open Google Calendar on a computer (or "Desktop site" in your phone's browser).
  2. Settings (gear) → under Settings for my calendars, pick your calendar.
  3. Integrate calendar → copy Secret address in iCal format.

Apple and Outlook calendars have a similar "publish" or "subscribe" link. Treat it like a password: anyone with it can see your calendar. Only you can see it here.
