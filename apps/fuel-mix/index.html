50:1 means 50 parts gas to 1 part oil. A bigger number means less oil. How to mix: pour the oil into the can first, add the gas, then cap it and shake. Mix only what you'll use in about a month, and use the oil the engine maker calls for. 4-stroke engines (separate oil fill) never get oil in the gas.
